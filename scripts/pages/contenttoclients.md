## How it works

Three steps: **Content, Conversations, Clients.**

**1. Content.** You set up one simple weekly routine that turns what you already know into content, in the formats you like: talking, writing or video. One session becomes many pieces. The routine is small enough that you're still doing it in week 12, not just week 2.

**2. Conversations.** Every piece ends with an easy invitation, so the right people raise their hand and start a chat with you. Content that only collects likes feels nice. Content that starts conversations is the kind that pays.

**3. Clients.** I show you how to move a chat towards a client in plain, low-pressure steps. You listen, you filter, you invite. No pushy pitching.
