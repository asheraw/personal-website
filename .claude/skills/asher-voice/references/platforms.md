## LinkedIn post

**Length:** Start around 1,000-1,300 characters. Go longer if the story needs it; don't pad to reach it.

**Shape:**
```
[Hook - line 1. Short, specific, earns the "see more" click]
[Line 2 - raises the stakes or deepens the curiosity]

[Body - one narrow slice of story + the point it earned]
[Short paragraphs; one-line beats only after a build-up]

[Close - callback to the hook, or a plain honest line]

[CTA - one easy question to one reader, or one clear action]

[P.S. - only if there's something real left over]
```

Only the first 2 or so lines show before the fold, so the hook and line 2 carry the whole post.

**Hooks in Asher's voice** (fill with his real material only):
- Admission: "I taught AI for months before I ever paid for it."
- Concrete number: "288 days. That's how long I didn't write."
- Scene already moving: "Dress rehearsal. One line. And it was gone."
- Direct question to the one reader: "When did you last post something without rewriting it 5 times?"
- Surprising fact he actually has, stated plainly.

**Avoid** the old template hooks that read as AI: "Most people get X wrong. Here's why.", "Stop doing X. Do Y instead.", "I spent X on Y. Here's what I found.", "Let's dive in", "I'm excited to share". Also avoid external links in the body (put them in a comment if needed) and engagement bait ("Agree?").

**Short-form rule:** one moment, told properly. If the draft summarises a whole story, narrow the slice.

## Instagram Reel script (60-90 seconds)

Write for the ear. Asher is a trained speaker and will say these words out loud, so read every line aloud in your head. If it trips the tongue, rewrite it.

```
HOOK (0-3s)
On-screen text: [3-6 bold words]
Spoken: [First line - a scene, an admission, or the viewer called out directly]

CONTEXT (3-12s)
[The exact situation or pain. 1-3 short sentences.]

BODY (12-75s)
[Either: one short story told in beats,
 or: up to 3-5 points, each = insight + why it matters, 1-2 sentences.]
[Note B-roll / text overlay per section.]

TURN (optional, ~30-40s)
[A natural pivot in his voice, e.g. "But this is the bit that got me."
 Not "Here's the one that changed everything."]

CTA (75-90s)
On-screen text: [FOLLOW / SAVE / COMMENT]
Spoken: [A specific reason to act, tied to this Reel]
```

- On-screen text reinforces the spoken line; it doesn't just repeat it.
- One idea every 3-5 seconds. Cut anything that slows the pace.
- Numerals on screen.

## YouTube script (8-20 minutes)

```
TITLE: [Under ~60 characters. Curiosity + specifics. Must be true to the content.]
THUMBNAIL CONCEPT: [One image that makes the promise visual]

HOOK (0-30s)
[Deliver on the title immediately: the result, the surprising fact, or the story's
 turning point as a teaser. Say what the viewer gets.]

INTRO (30-60s)
[Quick credibility anchor from real facts. Preview what's coming.
 Plant a reason to stay to the end.]

CHAPTER 1: [Title]
Opening line:
Story / explanation / example:
Practical application:
Transition into Chapter 2 (a question works well; it also invites comments):

[Repeat for each chapter]

CLOSE (final 60-90s)
[Callback to the hook's story or image.]
[For teaching videos only: a short spoken recap is fine, because viewers can't
 scroll back the way readers can. Keep it to one line per idea, and never
 "in conclusion".]
[One CTA only.]
```

## Newsletter

Treat it as a letter to one person. Same rules as a blog post, usually shorter (a single story or idea). Open with the story, not with "Hi everyone, this week...". Sign off the way Asher does; ask him if unsure.

## Captions and short posts

One idea. Hook in the first line. No hashtag walls; #KeepTryingUntil only if it belongs. A caption supports the image or video; it doesn't retell it.

## Bios and about pages

Use StoryBrand (see `story-frameworks.md`): the reader is the hero, Asher is the guide. Ask whether he wants first or third person before drafting, since this varies by platform. Credentials only from what he has supplied (marketing since 2008, brand work including Lexus, OCBC and Bayer, Nas Academy trainer, and so on). Never round up.

## Content batch

Default set, unless Asher asks for something else:
- 4 LinkedIn posts, one per week, each with a different job: educate, nurture, social proof, engage.
- 2 Reel scripts: one teaching, one story.
- 1 YouTube outline.

Spread them across his content pillars (storytelling, mindset, personal branding, AI, content creation, communication) and any campaign brief he provides.

**The batch trap:** batches are where invented stories creep in, because 7 pieces need 7 real moments. Before drafting, either:
- ask Asher for a quick story bank (one real moment per piece), or
- draft what you can from real material, and for the rest write the structure with a clear marker like `[NEED: a real moment when...]` instead of filling the gap.

A batch with 2 honest gaps is worth more than a complete batch with invented anecdotes.
