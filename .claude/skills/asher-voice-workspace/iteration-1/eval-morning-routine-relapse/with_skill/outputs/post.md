# Confession: I Keep Restarting the Same Morning Routine

Ten times this year. That's roughly how many times I've committed to waking up early to pray and journal before work.

Every time, it lasts about four days.

I wish I was exaggerating. I'm not. I've done this enough times now that I can see the shape of it before it even happens.

## What Actually Kicks It Off?

It's never a random Tuesday. It's always right after a spiritually "high" moment. Usually a church retreat.

I come back from one of those weekends feeling like a different person. Closer to God, more disciplined, finally sure of what my mornings are supposed to look like. So I set the alarm early. I get up. I pray. I journal. For a few days, it genuinely feels like this is the version of me that sticks around.

Then normal life kicks back in. Within a week, it's gone.

## Why Four Days, Specifically?

I don't fully know. But if I had to guess, four days is roughly how long the high lasts before it runs out.

The first morning, I'm not tired. I'm not fighting anything. The alarm goes off and I just get up, because I'm still riding the high from the retreat. By day four or five, that high has faded. And getting up early just... goes back to being hard. Same as it always was before the retreat.

That's the part I keep missing. I treat the high like it's the plan. It isn't. It's the thing that made the plan feel easy for a few days.

## Am I Chasing the Retreat or Building Something Real?

My honest guess, and it's still just a guess, is that I'm chasing the high instead of building something that can survive a normal Tuesday.

The retreat version of me isn't lying. He genuinely means it when he sets that alarm. But he's running on a high that was borrowed, not built. It was never going to last past the first week back at work.

A retreat isn't a sustainable state. It's a moment.

I keep mistaking the moment for the method.

## So Have I Fixed It?

No. I'd love to end this post with the three-step system that finally made it stick. I don't have one. Ten attempts in, and I still don't know what makes the eleventh one different.

What I do know is that the fix probably isn't "want it more." I've wanted it plenty. Ten times' worth of wanting it, actually. Whatever the fix is, it's probably quieter than a retreat, built for the version of me that isn't riding a high, just going through a normal week.

I don't know what that looks like yet. But at least now I know what I'm actually solving for. Not another retreat to chase. Something that can survive the Tuesday after.
