# Confession: I Almost Didn't Buy Claude Pro

I teach AI for a living. And I almost didn't pay for the AI tool that ended up saving me months.

For years, I've been off almost every software subscription. Cold turkey. Not a minimalist phase, not a budget thing. Scar tissue.

## What GrooveFunnels Did To Me

A while back, I signed up for GrooveFunnels. It promised to replace WordPress. One platform, everything in it, no more juggling.

It didn't replace WordPress. It just added new bugs next to the old ones.

Then it messed up an affiliate commission I was owed. Not a huge sum. But that was the moment "buggy platform" turned into "I don't trust you with my money."

So I stopped. Not just GrooveFunnels. Anything recurring. If it had a monthly charge on it, I found the free version, or I found a way to live without it.

## Even the AI Tools I Teach People to Use?

Yeah. Even those.

Which is a bit much when you think about it, because I stand in front of people and teach AI classes for a living. And behind the scenes, I was building my own new website almost entirely on free tools.

My main one was GLM, over at z.ai. Free, decent, got me somewhere.

And the whole time, I was waiting. Waiting for Kimi K3, an open-source model, to release, so I could use it and never have to think about a subscription at all.

## Then Kimi K3 Just Didn't Open Up

It never released to consumers. I don't know if it will. I just know I couldn't keep waiting on it, so I finally caved and got Claude Pro.

After years of cold turkey, that felt like a bigger decision than it should have.

## What Happened Next

Within the first day, I had a proper CMS built. Backups. A blog page. And more than that, honestly.

More progress in one day than I'd made in months on the free tools.

Months. One day.

## So Was Cold Turkey the Right Call?

GrooveFunnels was still a real burn, and I don't think the caution it left me with was wrong. I still check twice before handing anyone my card details for something recurring.

But it also cost me. Actual months of building this site slower than I needed to, because I was more scared of a repeat than I was curious about what a good tool could actually do for me.

Cold turkey kept me safe from GrooveFunnels. It also kept me from Claude Pro for way longer than it should have. Funny how the discipline and the mistake can look exactly the same from the inside.

P.S. I'm well aware of the irony of an AI trainer being the last person in the room to pay for AI. If any of my students are reading this… yeah, I know.
